# Product Design

Design products from scratch without an existing design reference.

## Responsibilities

- Define a consistent Design System: typography, color palette, spacing scale, and visual tokens.
- Establish clear visual hierarchy.
- Design customer-facing Kiosk interfaces and administrative dashboards when requested.
- Support responsive layouts across device sizes.
- Support both RTL and LTR layouts.
- Define Loading, Empty, Error, and Success states for every view that fetches or mutates data.
- Prioritize usability, accessibility, and touch-friendly interactions.

## Rules

- Do not invent or implement designs unless explicitly requested.
- Avoid unnecessary visual complexity.
- Maintain consistency across all interfaces (reuse tokens and patterns rather than introducing one-off styles).
- Base design decisions on stated product requirements, not personal preference.
